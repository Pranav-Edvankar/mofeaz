## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Design Language

The MOFEAZ packaging architecture is inspired by the intersection of **old-world European apothecary craft**, **high-fashion editorial typography**, and **rigorous clinical endocrinology**. 

### Core Design Rules
1. **Hero Statement Typography**: `Bodoni Moda` (Editorial Serif, high contrast, tracking 0.02em - 0.08em).
2. **Functional & Technical Typography**: `Montserrat` (Clean Geometric Sans-Serif, uppercase, tracking 0.15em - 0.35em).
3. **Tactile Haptic Feel**: Every consumer touchpoint must utilize soft-touch matte velvet coating contrasted with hot-stamped metallic foil and tactile blind debossing.
4. **Color Coding by Phase**:
   - **Phase 01 (Follicular // Days 1 - 14)**: Deep Forest Green & Luxor Gold.
   - **Phase 02 (Luteal // Days 15 - 28)**: Earthy Terracotta Clay, Warm Sand, & Copper Foil.
   - **Signature Kit**: Unbleached Natural Textured Linen with Espresso debossing.

## 4. Special Print Finishes & Tooling

1. **Soft-Touch Matte Velvet Lamination**:
   - Applied to the exterior of all folding carton boxes (Follicular, Luteal, and Coffee bars).
   - Provides an ultra-premium, tactile, anti-glare peach-skin feel that resists fingerprint oils.
2. **Precision Hot Foil Stamping (Hot Stamping Die)**:
   - Tooling: CNC-milled 0.25 in magnesium or brass stamping dies.
   - Foil: Kurz Luxor 420 (Gold) and Kurz Luxor 425 (Copper).
   - Application: Brand name `MOFEAZ`, Phase indicators, botanical seed outlines, and luxury borders.
3. **Multilevel Blind Debossing**:
   - Applied to the 28-day cycle calendar wheel on the rigid kit box cover.
   - Creates a sunken relief impression without color, offering an authentic astronomical tactile experience.
4. **Spot High-Gloss UV Varnish**:
   - Selectively applied over the botanical seed illustrations (`Pepitas`, `Sesame`, `Flaxseed`) to contrast against the soft-touch matte background.
5. **Food-Contact Compliance**:
   - Direct/indirect inks must comply with **FSSAI Food Contact Regulations**, **EU Regulation 1935/2004**, and **Swiss Ordinance on Food Contact Materials**.
   - Use vegetable-oil based, low-migration, mineral-oil-free (MOSH/MOAH-free) offset litho inks.
