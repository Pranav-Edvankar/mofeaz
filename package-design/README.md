## 1. Dielines & Production Nets (`/dielines`)

Each dieline is drafted according to standard industrial packaging conventions:
- **Solid Magenta (`#E6007E`)**: Die Cut Line (blade cut boundary).
- **Dashed Cyan (`#00A0E9`)**: Crease / Score Line (machine fold lines).
- **Dotted Green (`#00B050`)**: Bleed Margin (3.0 mm exterior safety zone).
- **Included Content**: Front panel hero branding, back panel FSSAI regulatory nutrition facts table, barcode zone, side spine typography, batch date zones, and glue tabs.

All files can be opened and edited directly in **Adobe Illustrator**, **Adobe InDesign**, **Figma**, **CorelDraw**, or sent directly to carton printers.
